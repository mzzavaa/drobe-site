Secondhand is the most sustainable way to buy clothes and the hardest way to
judge them. Everything that makes online shopping difficult is worse here.

{{< img src="photo-boutique-interior.webp" caption="One of each, no second size, and usually no returns." >}}

## What makes it harder than retail

{{< pair
  problem="**One of each.** There is no second size to order. You buy it or you lose it, usually within the hour on a resale app."
  fix="Deciding faster is only possible if you can decide accurately. Measurements against a known body beat a guess made under time pressure." >}}

{{< pair
  problem="**Vintage sizing is a different system.** A 1970s 12 is not a current 12. Neither is a US 12 or a Japanese one. The label is close to meaningless."
  fix="Sellers list flat measurements precisely because labels do not travel. Those map onto your proportions; the label never did." >}}

{{< pair
  problem="**The photograph is a garment on a hanger in someone's hallway**, badly lit, sometimes on a carpet."
  fix="A flat-lay is exactly the input a try-on wants. The bad photo is a problem for judging the colour, not for judging the fit." >}}

{{< pair
  problem="**Final sale.** No returns, and postage back would cost more than the item."
  fix="The cost of a wrong guess is the whole point. This is where being able to check is worth the most." >}}

{{< pair
  problem="**You are in a charity shop with a queue for one changing room**, holding four things."
  fix="Your wardrobe is in your pocket. Whether it goes with anything you own is answerable while you are standing there." >}}

{{< pair
  problem="**You buy the thing you like rather than the thing you will wear**, and it joins the pile of good decisions you never put on."
  fix="Try it against what you already own. If nothing pairs with it, that is the answer, and it cost nothing to find out." >}}

## Why this matters beyond the money

Extending the life of a garment is the single largest lever most people have on
the environmental cost of their clothes. Buying secondhand is the cheap version
of that lever, and the main thing stopping people is that it is hard to judge and
often unreturnable.

Reduce the guessing and secondhand stops being a gamble.
