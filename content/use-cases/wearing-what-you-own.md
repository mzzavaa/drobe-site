Most wardrobe advice ends in a purchase. This one does not. The largest
improvement available to nearly everyone is wearing the clothes they already
bought, and the reason they do not is that they cannot see them.

## The honest version of sustainability

The most sustainable garment is one you already own and actually wear. Everything
else - recycled fibres, ethical supply chains, resale - matters, but is downstream
of the plain fact that most clothing is thrown away long before it wears out,
and often barely worn at all.

A wardrobe you can see is a wardrobe you use. That is the whole mechanism.
