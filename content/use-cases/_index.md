title: "Use cases"
eyebrow: "Real days"
description: "The situations people actually get caught by, grouped by when they happen."
---

Every one of these starts from something that goes wrong for reasons that have
nothing to do with fashion. You could not see what you owned. Nobody told you
what the dress code meant. The weather was different at the other end of the
flight. The size on the label meant nothing.

They are grouped by when the situation happens rather than by which feature it
exercises:

- **Every day** is the one almost everybody has, and the one that costs the most
  time in total.
- **Occasions with rules** covers the events where the rules are unwritten, or
  technical, or both.
- **Travel** is where two sets of requirements have to fit in one bag.
- **Buying, swapping and letting go** is the money and the waste: not ordering
  three sizes, judging a secondhand listing, and actually getting the bag by the
  door out of the house.
