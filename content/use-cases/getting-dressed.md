Nobody needs an app to look good at a wedding once a year. The thing that
actually costs you is Tuesday, and the Tuesday after that.

{{< img src="photo-folded-grid.webp" caption="What a wardrobe looks like when you can see all of it at once. This is the state the app is trying to get you to, not a tidying instruction." >}}

## What the morning actually looks like

You open the wardrobe. You are looking at the sides of about forty hangers.
The front-facing item is whatever you wore last. You reach past everything to
the same jeans and the same jumper, not because you prefer them but because
they are the only two things you can currently see and be certain are clean.

Ten minutes goes on this. Some of it is decision, most of it is search.

## What changes

Not that you dress better. That you stop spending the ten minutes, and that the
thirty-five items you own but never wear come back into rotation because you can
finally see them.
