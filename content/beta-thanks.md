We have sent you an email with a confirmation button. Click it and you are on
the beta waitlist. Until then you are not, which is how it should be - nobody
gets added to a list by someone else typing in their address.

Nothing arrived after a few minutes? Check the spam or promotions folder for the
subject line "DROBE beta: please confirm your spot on the waitlist".

In the meantime, the [roadmap](/product/roadmap/) says what beta will and will
not include.
