## They are generated, not photographed

Every mood and illustrative image on this site was produced with **Google
NotebookLM / Gemini**. None of them is a photograph. The rooms do not exist, the
garments were never made, and any person shown is not a real person.

This is stated because artificial content should be disclosed to the people
looking at it, and because it is easy to forget once an image is sitting on a
page looking like a photograph.

## The exception: the screenshots

The app screenshots are not generated. They are captures of the running build at
390, 768 and 1280 pixels wide. The animated avatar on the home page is the
demo's own asset.

Where you see an interface on this site, it is the interface.

## What we will not do

- Present a generated image as a photograph of a real person, place or product
- Show a generated garment as an item you could buy
- Use a generated face in a testimonial, review or endorsement
- Remove the credit when an image is used elsewhere

## It is written into the files

Each image in the library carries its origin in its own metadata: `Artist`,
`Copyright` and `Software` all name the tool, and `ImageDescription` says what it
shows. A page can be edited and a caption can be dropped. Metadata travels with
the file.

## When there are real photographs

There will be. As the product reaches real wardrobes, generated imagery gets
replaced with photographs of actual clothes in actual homes. When a picture on
this page is a photograph, this page will say so and the credit will change.
