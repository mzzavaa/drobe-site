## Where it actually gets hard

**Sizing is not a number.** The same nominal size varies by several centimetres
between brands, and often between two items from the same brand. That is most of
why online returns run so high. An avatar with your actual proportions is worth
more here than almost anywhere.

**Each garment carries a dozen facts.** Whether it is opaque under bright light.
Whether it rides up when you sit. Whether it creases in a car. Whether it needs a
particular bra, or cannot take one at all. Whether the neckline gives a lapel mic
anything to clip to. Each fact is learned once, usually the hard way, and then
has to be remembered forever. There is no reason for it to live in your memory
rather than on the item.

**Your body is not the same shape every week.** Fit changes across a cycle. The
waistband that is comfortable on one day is not on another, and the trousers you
reach for on a bad day are a specific known pair, which you re-derive every
month. The same holds through pregnancy and after it, through weight change,
illness, injury, a cast, or simply a decade. A digital body that is current turns
"does this still fit" from a question into a fact.

**The rules are unwritten and still enforced.** Not white at a wedding, and not
cream or ivory either. Covered shoulders at that site visit. Too short for that
office, and nobody will define too short. Every one of them is unstated, and
every one is noticed.
