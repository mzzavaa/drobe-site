## The short version

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking, and loads nothing
from other servers: styles, images and video are all served from here. The only
personal data it collects is what you type into the beta waitlist form.

## The beta waitlist

**What is collected.** Your first name and email address, and nothing else.

**What it is for.** Telling you when the DROBE beta opens and sending you
emails about the beta. It is not sold, shared or used for anything else.

**Legal basis.** Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You give it by submitting
the form and then clicking the confirmation link in the email that follows
(double opt-in). Until you click that link you are not on the list.

**Who processes it.** The form sends your details to the email platform
**Kajabi** (Kajabi, LLC, USA), which runs the mailing list on behalf of the
operator. This involves a transfer of your data to the United States. After
you submit, the confirmation page you see is served by Kajabi on
lindamohamed.com, where the
[lindamohamed.com privacy policy](https://www.lindamohamed.com/privacy-policy)
applies.

**How long it is kept.** Until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion. Every
email has an unsubscribe link, and withdrawing consent does not affect emails
already sent.

## Hosting and server logs

The site is static and hosted on **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any
web host, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data, including
your IP address, in order to deliver the pages and keep the service secure.
That is governed by
[GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement)
and involves a transfer to the United States. The operator has no access to
those logs. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running the site securely
(Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Links to other sites

Links that leave this site are followed only when you click them. The other
site's own privacy policy then applies.
